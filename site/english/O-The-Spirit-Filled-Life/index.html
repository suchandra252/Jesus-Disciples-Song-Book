    O The Spirit Filled Life

    Song: O The Spirit Filled Life

    Song Meaning

    Stanza 1
    O the Spirit filled life; is it thine, is it thine?
    Is thy soul wholly filled with the Spirit Divine?
    O thou child of the King, has He fallen on thee?
    Does He reign in thy soul, so that all men may see
    The dear Savior’s blest image reflected in thee?

    Meaning:
    The song opens with searching, personal questions that challenge the believer. It asks whether we truly possess a Spirit-filled life — one where the Holy Spirit completely controls and fills the soul. The evidence is whether Christ’s character is visibly reflected in us for others to see.

    Stanza 2
    Has He swept through thy soul like the waves of the sea?
    Does the Spirit of God daily rest upon thee?
    Does He sweeten thy life, does He keep thee from care?
    Does He guide thee and bless thee in answer to prayer?
    Is it joy to be led of the Lord anywhere?

    Meaning:
    This stanza describes the powerful and daily effects of the Holy Spirit: like mighty waves cleansing and filling the soul, bringing sweetness, peace, guidance, answered prayer, and joyful willingness to follow God anywhere.

    Stanza 3
    Is His service thy choice, and is sacrifice sweet?
    Is the doing His will both thy drink and thy meat?
    Dost thou run at His bidding with glad eager feet?

    Meaning:
    These lines examine our attitude toward serving God. A Spirit-filled life makes obedience a delight, sacrifice feel sweet, and doing God’s will as satisfying as food and drink, with eager readiness to obey.

    Stanza 4
    Has He freed thee from self and from all of thy greed?
    Dost thou hasten to succor thy brother in need?
    As a soldier of Christ dost thou hardness endure?
    Is thy hope in the Lord everlasting and sure?
    Hast thou patience and meekness, art tender and pure?

    Meaning:
    This stanza lists the practical fruits of being filled with the Spirit: freedom from selfishness and greed, quick compassion for others, endurance in hardship, firm hope in God, and the growth of Christ-like qualities such as patience, meekness, tenderness, and purity.

    Stanza 5
    O the Spirit filled life may be thine, may be thine,
    In thy soul evermore the Shekinah may shine;
    It is thine to live with the tempests all stilled,
    It is thine with the blessed Holy Ghost to be filled;
    It is thine, even thine, for thy Lord has so willed.

    Meaning:
    The song ends on a strong note of hope and invitation. The Spirit-filled life is available to every believer. God’s glory (“Shekinah”) can shine continually in our hearts, bringing peace amid storms, because the Lord Himself desires and wills this for us.

    Summary of Song
    This classic hymn by Christmas Evans is a probing and encouraging call to experience the fullness of the Holy Spirit. Through a series of honest, self-examining questions, it reveals what a truly Spirit-filled life looks like — marked by God’s presence, victory over self, joyful obedience, Christ-like character, and inner peace. The song moves from challenge to wonderful assurance that this blessed life is available to every child of God who desires it. It is ideal for personal reflection, prayer meetings, or revival services when the church seeks a deeper work of the Holy Spirit.
