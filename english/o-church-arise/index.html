    📝 Lyrics

    “O Church, arise, and put your armor on;
    Hear the call of Christ our Captain.
    For now the weak can say that they are strong
    In the strength that God has given.”

    Verse Explanation in English Explanation in Telugu
    Verse 1 A call to the Church to put on the whole armor of God, standing against deception with divine strength. The body of Christ is summoned to wake up and take up God's spiritual armor.     Verse 2 Going out into spiritual warfare with love for captive souls, wielding the healing sword of God's Word.     🌟 Summary of Song

    O Church, Arise! is a modern classic hymn of Christian discipleship and spiritual warfare composed by Keith Getty and Stuart Townend. It rallies the body of Christ to advance in spiritual victory—not with earthly weapons, but clothed in divine armor, preaching truth, showing love to captive souls, and drawing unwavering confidence from Christ's decisive victory at Calvary and the empty tomb.
