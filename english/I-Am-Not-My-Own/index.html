    Song Meaning

    Stanza 1

    The One who made the heavens made my heart and soul
    Before I drew a breath, I was loved and known
    I am His creation, the Maker's masterpiece
    And all that He designs will be done in me

    Meaning:
    This verse establishes our identity as God’s deliberate creation. The same God who created the universe personally formed us and loved us even before we existed. We are not accidents but His masterpiece, and He will complete His good purposes in our lives.

    Stanza 2

    My body is a temple of the Living God
    I'll worship in this house that His blood has bought
    As I bear His image, oh may I not profane
    The holiness I hold in this earthly frame

    Meaning:
    Drawing from 1 Corinthians 6:19-20, this stanza reminds us that our bodies belong to God and are sacred temples purchased by Christ’s blood. We are called to honour Him by living holy lives that reflect His image without defiling what He has made holy.

    Chorus

    I belong to the Lord, oh I am not my own
    I belong to the Lord, I am not my own
    I will honor Him for this I know
    I belong to the Lord, I am not my own

    Meaning:
    The powerful, repeated chorus is a joyful declaration of belonging. Because we were bought with a price, we are not our own. This truth frees us to live for God’s honour and glory rather than for ourselves.

    Stanza 3

    And if He has redeemed me, I am not my own
    The measure of my worth is His love alone
    He declares my standing, and He declares my state
    So I will know myself by the name He gave

    Meaning:
    Redemption by Christ confirms our complete belonging to Him. Our value is not based on performance, appearance, or achievements but solely on His unchanging love. We find our true identity in the names and standing God declares over us.

    Stanza 4

    I am not my own and now my heart is free
    O Maker come and make what You will of me
    There is nothing broken that You cannot repair
    So Lord, I leave my life in Your loving care

    Meaning:
    Surrendering ownership to God brings true freedom. The singer invites the Lord (the Maker) to shape their life as He desires, trusting that He can heal and restore anything broken, and willingly placing their entire life into His caring hands.

    Summary

    This beautiful modern hymn is a powerful declaration of belonging to God and finding true identity and freedom in Him. It reminds us that we are lovingly created, redeemed by Christ’s blood, and called to live as holy temples that honour the Lord.

    With its clear biblical truth and tender melody, the song encourages complete surrender — “I am not my own” — leading to a life shaped by God’s loving design, healing, and purpose.

    It is especially meaningful for personal devotion, teaching on identity in Christ, or worship services focused on surrender and holiness.
