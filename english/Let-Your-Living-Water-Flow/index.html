    Song Meaning

    Stanza 1
    Let Your Living Water Flow Over My Soul,
    Let Your Holy Spirit Come And Take Control,
    Of Every Situation That Has Troubled My Mind,
    All My Cares And Burdens On To You I Roll.

    Meaning:
    This opening stanza is a heartfelt prayer inviting the Holy Spirit’s refreshing presence into the singer’s life. “Living Water” refers to the life-giving, cleansing power of the Holy Spirit that Jesus promised. The singer asks the Spirit to take full control over every troubling situation and worry, then deliberately rolls all cares and burdens onto God, choosing to release anxiety and fully trust Him instead of carrying the weight alone.

    Chorus / Response
    Jesus, Jesus, Jesus.
    Father, Father, Father

    Meaning:
    The simple, repeated calling of “Jesus, Jesus, Jesus” and “Father, Father, Father” expresses intimate love, dependence, and worship. It creates a direct, personal conversation with God, turning the heart away from problems and toward the loving presence of the Trinity in a gentle yet powerful way.

    Stanza 2
    Come Now Holy Spirit And Take Control,
    Hold Me In Your Loving Arms And Make Me Whole,
    Wipe Away All Doubt And Fear And Take My Pride,
    Draw Me To Your Love And Keep Me By Your Side.

    Meaning:
    Here the prayer becomes more personal and healing-focused. The singer asks the Holy Spirit to embrace them with love, bring complete wholeness, remove doubts, fears, and pride, and draw them closer to God’s love. It is a plea for inner transformation and ongoing closeness with the Lord, surrendering everything that hinders the relationship.

    Stanza 3
    Give Your Life To Jesus, Let Him Fill Your Soul,
    Let Him Take You In His Arms And Make You Whole,
    As You Give Your Life To Him, He’ll Set You Free,
    You Will Live And Reign With Him Eternally.

    Meaning:
    This stanza shifts into an invitation and gospel message. It calls the listener (and the singer) to surrender their life fully to Jesus so He can fill, heal, and free them. It promises true freedom from sin and bondage, along with the eternal hope of living and reigning with Christ forever, highlighting the life-changing result of giving everything to Him.

    Final Stanza (Repeat of Stanza 1)
    Let Your Living Water Flow Over My Soul,
    Let Your Holy Spirit Come And Take Control,
    Of Every Situation That Has Troubled My Mind,
    All My Cares And Burdens On To You I Roll.

    Meaning:
    Repeating the first stanza brings the song full circle. It reinforces that the prayer for the Holy Spirit’s flow and control is not a one-time request but a continual need, closing the song on the same note of surrender and trust.

    Summary of Song
    This is a gentle, Spirit-filled worship prayer that invites the Holy Spirit to flow like living water over the soul, bringing refreshment, control, healing, and freedom. The singer releases all worries, fears, and pride to God while seeking wholeness and closeness with Him, then extends an invitation to fully surrender one’s life to Jesus for eternal life. With its simple, repetitive structure and warm melody, the song creates an atmosphere of intimate surrender, making it ideal for personal devotion, healing, or inviting the Holy Spirit’s presence during worship.
