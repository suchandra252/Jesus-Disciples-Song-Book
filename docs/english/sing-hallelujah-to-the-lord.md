## Song Meaning

| Stanza | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Chorus**<br>Sing Hallelujah to the Lord...<br>Sing Hallelujah to the Lord! | A joyful, repetitive call to praise God with the word “Hallelujah” (Praise the Lord). It sets a celebratory tone inviting everyone to worship the Lord together. |
| **Verse 1**<br>Jesus has conquered sin and death... | This verse proclaims the victory of Jesus over sin and death through His crucifixion and resurrection — the foundation of the Christian faith. |
| **Verse 2**<br>Jesus has risen from the dead... | It celebrates the resurrection of Jesus, affirming that He is alive and death has been defeated. |
| **Verse 3**<br>Jesus is Lord of heav’n and earth... | Declares the lordship of Jesus over all creation — heaven and earth — acknowledging His supreme authority. |
| **Verse 4**<br>Jesus is coming back again!... | A powerful declaration of hope in the Second Coming of Christ, encouraging believers to live in expectant readiness. |

## Summary of Song

This uplifting and triumphant worship song is a classic declaration of praise and victory in Jesus. Through simple, repeatable lines, it proclaims key truths of the gospel: Christ’s victory over sin and death, His resurrection, His lordship over all, and His promised return. With its joyful “Hallelujah” chorus, the song creates a powerful atmosphere of celebration and faith. It is perfect for Easter, revival meetings, praise gatherings, or any time the church wants to exalt the risen and coming Lord.
