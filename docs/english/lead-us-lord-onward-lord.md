## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse 1

> Lead us Lord, onward Lord,  
> As our eyes are fixed on You.  
> Plant our footsteps firmly Lord to walk with You.  
> Take us Lord, further Lord as our hands reach out to You.  
> Lift us higher Lord our hearts reach out to You.

### Chorus

> Hand in hand with each other  
> We will go in Jesus' Name.  
> With our hearts held together  
> Lord Your promises we claim.  
> Take us Lord, higher Lord  
> As our eyes look up to You.  
> Move us on in step with You,  
> Lord lead us on.

### Verse 2

> Fill us Lord, touch us Lord  
> Make our hearts with Yours to beat.  
> Reaching out in love we'll touch a world in need.  
> Show us Lord, teach us Lord  
> Give direction for the way.  
> Moving onward Lord to serve from day to day!

## Summary of Song

This inspiring worship song is a heartfelt prayer for God’s leadership and guidance as believers move forward together in unity. It combines personal surrender (fixed eyes on the Lord, hearts reaching out to Him) with corporate commitment (hand in hand, serving the world). Based on the desire to walk closely with God and fulfil His purposes, the song encourages the church to advance in step with the Lord, claiming His promises while touching lives with His love. It is ideal for dedication services, missions emphasis, or times of seeking God’s direction for the church.
