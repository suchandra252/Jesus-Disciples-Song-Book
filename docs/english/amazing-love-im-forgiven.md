## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse 1

> I'm forgiven, because You were forsaken,  
> I'm accepted, You were condemned.  
> I'm alive and well, Your spirit is within me,  
> Because You died and rose again. (2)

### Chorus

> Amazing love, how can it be  
> That You, my King, would die for me?  
> Amazing love, I know it's true  
> And it's my joy to honor You,  
> In all I do, I honor You.

### Bridge

> You are my King,  
> Jesus, You are my King.  
> You are my King,  
> Jesus, You are my King.

## Song Meaning

| Stanza | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Verse 1**<br>I'm forgiven, because You were forsaken,<br>I'm accepted, You were condemned.<br>I'm alive and well, Your spirit is within me,<br>Because You died and rose again. | This powerful verse contrasts what Jesus suffered for us (forsaken, condemned, died) with what we receive because of it (forgiven, accepted, alive with His Spirit). It beautifully summarizes the exchange at the cross. |
| **Chorus**<br>Amazing love, how can it be<br>That You, my King, would die for me?<br>Amazing love, I know it's true<br>And it's my joy to honor You,<br>In all I do, I honor You. | The chorus expresses awe at the wonder of God’s love — that the King of kings would die for us. It turns this amazement into a joyful commitment to live a life that honors Him in everything. |
| **Bridge**<br>You are my King,<br>Jesus, You are my King. | A simple, repeated declaration of Jesus’ lordship. It affirms that He is not only Saviour but also the rightful King over our lives. |

## Summary of Song

This well-loved modern worship song by Billy James Foote is a heartfelt celebration of God’s amazing love displayed through the cross. It contrasts Jesus’ suffering with the wonderful benefits we receive — forgiveness, acceptance, and new life through the Holy Spirit. With its simple yet powerful lyrics and emotional melody, the song leads worshippers into awe, gratitude, and a renewed commitment to honor Jesus as King. It is perfect for communion services, Easter, or any time of personal reflection on the cross.
