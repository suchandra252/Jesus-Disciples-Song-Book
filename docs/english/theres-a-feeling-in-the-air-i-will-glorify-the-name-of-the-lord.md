## Summary of Song

This energetic and faith-filled worship song creates a powerful atmosphere of expectancy. It declares that God’s resurrection power is moving in the present moment and openly commits to glorifying the name of the Lord. With its strong emphasis on God’s tangible presence and victorious power, the song stirs believers to worship with boldness and anticipation. It is especially suitable for revival meetings, praise and worship services, or any gathering where the church desires to see God move powerfully.
