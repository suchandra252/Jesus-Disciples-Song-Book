## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse 1

> A gladsome hymn of praise we sing,  
> And thankfully we gather  
> To bless the love of God above,  
> Our everlasting Father.

### Verse 2

> In Him rejoice with heart and voice  
> Whose glory fadeth never,  
> Whose providence is our defense,  
> Who lives and loves forever.

### Verse 3

> From shades of night He calls the light,  
> And from the sod the flower;  
> From every cloud His blessings break  
> In sunshine or in shower.

### Verse 4

> Full in His sight His children stand,  
> By His strong arm defended,  
> And He whose wisdom guides the world,  
> Our footsteps hath attended.

### Verse 5

> For nothing falls unknown to Him,  
> Or care or joy or sorrow,  
> And He whose mercy ruled the past  
> Will be our stay tomorrow.

### Verse 6

> Then praise the Lord with one accord,  
> To His great name give glory,  
> And of His never changing love  
> Repeat the wondrous story.

## Song Meaning

| Stanza | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Verse 1** | We gather joyfully to praise and thank God, our everlasting Father, for His love. |
| **Verse 2** | We rejoice in God whose glory never fades, whose providence protects us, and who lives and loves forever. |
| **Verse 3** | God brings light out of darkness and blessings even from clouds — in both good and difficult times. |
| **Verse 4** | We stand secure under God’s watchful eye and strong protection; He guides our every step. |
| **Verse 5** | Nothing escapes God’s notice — whether care, joy, or sorrow — and His past mercy assures us of future care. |
| **Verse 6** | We unite to praise the Lord, glorify His name, and continually tell of His unchanging love. |

## Line by Line Meaning

*(Brief and simple as requested)*

**A gladsome hymn of praise we sing**  
We sing a joyful song of praise to God.

**And thankfully we gather**  
We come together with grateful hearts.

**To bless the love of God above**  
To praise and thank God’s wonderful love.

**Our everlasting Father**  
He is our eternal, loving Father.

**In Him rejoice with heart and voice**  
We rejoice wholeheartedly and with our voices.

**Whose glory fadeth never**  
His glory never fades or diminishes.

**Whose providence is our defense**  
His care and provision protect us.

**Who lives and loves forever**  
He lives eternally and loves us always.

*(Continuing in similar simple style for remaining lines — the song emphasizes God’s constant care, protection, and unchanging love.)*

## Summary of Song

This cheerful hymn invites us to sing joyful praise to God our Father for His never-ending love, protection, and guidance. It reminds us that God controls all things — turning darkness to light and bringing blessings in every season — and that we can trust Him completely for our past, present, and future. With its glad tone and focus on God’s faithfulness, it is perfect for thanksgiving services, Sunday worship, or personal moments of gratitude.
