# I Do Not Know How Long I’ll Live

**Copyright Information:** Zac Poonen, Public Domain. This recording was made at Christian Fellowship Church, Bangalore, India.

## Lyrics

I do not know how long I’ll live,  
But while I live, Lord, let me give,  
Some comfort to someone in need,  
By smile or nod, kind word or deed.  

**Chorus:**  
I only want to love like Thee,  
Lord help me live on earth like Thee (2)  

Lord, help me do whate’er I can,  
To ease things for my fellow man,  
I only want to do my part,  
To lift a tired, and weary heart.  

If I can make folks love again,  
Then I will not have lived in vain,  
And I’ll not care how long I live,  
If I can love and give and give.

## Song Meaning

| Stanza | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Stanza 1**<br>I do not know how long I’ll live,<br>But while I live, Lord, let me give,<br>Some comfort to someone in need,<br>By smile or nod, kind word or deed. | The song begins with humble acknowledgment of life’s uncertainty — we don’t know how many days we have left. Instead of worrying about the length of life, the singer prays for a meaningful life: to bring comfort to others through simple, everyday acts of kindness like a smile, nod, encouraging word, or helpful deed. |
| **Chorus**<br>I only want to love like Thee,<br>Lord help me live on earth like Thee | This is the heartfelt prayer at the centre of the song. The singer’s deepest desire is to love and live exactly like Jesus — reflecting His compassion, humility, and self-giving love in daily life. |
| **Stanza 2**<br>Lord, help me do whate’er I can,<br>To ease things for my fellow man,<br>I only want to do my part,<br>To lift a tired, and weary heart. | This stanza expresses a willing spirit to help in any possible way — to lighten the burdens of others and bring encouragement to those who are tired or discouraged. It is a prayer for usefulness and practical love toward fellow human beings. |
| **Stanza 3**<br>If I can make folks love again,<br>Then I will not have lived in vain,<br>And I’ll not care how long I live,<br>If I can love and give and give. | The closing stanza gives the ultimate perspective on life. If the singer can help restore love in people’s hearts, then his life will have true meaning and purpose. Length of life becomes unimportant; what matters is the ability to keep loving and giving like Christ until the end. |

## Summary of Song

This gentle and convicting hymn by Zac Poonen is a sincere prayer for a life of Christ-like love and usefulness. It acknowledges the uncertainty of our lifespan while focusing on making every remaining day count by bringing comfort, encouragement, and love to others. The repeated chorus expresses the core desire: to live and love exactly as Jesus did. The song challenges believers to move beyond self-centred living and instead pour out simple, practical kindness, knowing that a life spent loving and giving is never lived in vain. It is especially meaningful for personal devotion, self-examination, or services focused on Christian service and compassion.
