## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse 1

> I do not know how long I'll live,  
> But while I live, Lord, let me give,  
> Some comfort to someone in need,  
> By smile or nod, kind word or deed.

### Chorus

> I only want to love like Thee,  
> Lord help me live on earth like Thee.  
> *(Repeat)*

### Verse 2

> Lord, help me do whate'er I can,  
> To ease things for my fellow man,  
> I only want to do my part,  
> To lift a tired and weary heart.

### Verse 3

> If I can make folks love again,  
> Then I will not have lived in vain,  
> And I'll not care how long I live,  
> If I can love and give and give.

## 💡 Song Meaning

=== "Verse 1"

    Life is uncertain, but the purpose of life is not.  
    The writer prays that every remaining day may bring comfort to others through simple acts of kindness, encouragement, and compassion.

=== "Chorus"

    The central prayer of the song.

    The desire is not merely to do good works but to **love exactly as Jesus loved** and to reflect His character in everyday life.

=== "Verse 2"

    This verse expresses a willingness to serve others in practical ways.

    The focus is on lifting burdens, encouraging the discouraged, and helping fellow believers and neighbours.

=== "Verse 3"

    True success is not measured by the length of life but by the amount of love we share.

    A life that restores love in others is never wasted.
