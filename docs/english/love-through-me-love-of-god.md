## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse 1

> Love through me, Love of God;  
> There is no love in me.  
> O Fire of love, light Thou the love  
> That burns perpetually.

### Verse 2

> Flow through me, Peace of God;  
> Calm River, flow until  
> No wind can blow, no current stir  
> A ripple of self-will.

### Verse 3

> Shine through me, Joy of God;  
> Make me like Thy clear air  
> That Thou dost pour Thy colors through,  
> As though it were not there.

### Verse 4

> O blessed Love of God,  
> That all may taste and see  
> How good Thou art, once more I pray:  
> Love through me—even me.

## Song Meaning

| Stanza | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Verse 1**<br>Love through me, Love of God;<br>There is no love in me.<br>O Fire of love, light Thou the love<br>That burns perpetually. | The singer acknowledges their own lack of true love and prays that God’s divine love would flow through them like an unquenchable fire, enabling them to love others with God’s love. |
| **Verse 2**<br>Flow through me, Peace of God;<br>Calm River, flow until<br>No wind can blow, no current stir<br>A ripple of self-will. | A beautiful prayer for God’s peace to flow like a calm river, completely removing any self-will or disturbance so that only God’s peace remains visible. |
| **Verse 3**<br>Shine through me, Joy of God;<br>Make me like Thy clear air<br>That Thou dost pour Thy colors through,<br>As though it were not there. | The singer asks to become like clear, invisible air — so transparent that only God’s joy shines through, with no self getting in the way. |
| **Verse 4**<br>O blessed Love of God,<br>That all may taste and see<br>How good Thou art, once more I pray:<br>Love through me—even me. | A final, humble plea that God’s love would flow through the singer so others can experience how good God is. It emphasises personal surrender (“even me”). |

## Summary of Song

This deeply sincere prayer song by Amy Carmichael is a cry for God to use the believer as a clean channel for His own love, peace, and joy. Each verse beautifully asks God to flow through the singer — removing self, pride, and natural limitations — so that others may taste and see the goodness of God. With its honest self-examination and passionate longing, the song is a powerful challenge and encouragement for anyone desiring to live a transparent, Christ-reflecting life. It is especially meaningful for personal devotion and consecration.
