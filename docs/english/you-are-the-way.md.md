## <span style="color:#8B0000;">🌟 Summary of Song</span>

This powerful song declares the gospel: we were dead in our sins, but God by His grace called us and opened our eyes. Jesus is the only Way, the only Truth, and the only Life. He emptied Himself, died for us, and is now exalted. Only His name can save. 🙌
