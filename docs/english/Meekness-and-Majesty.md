**_Song Meaning_**

**Stanza 1**  
Meekness and majesty manhood and deity  
In perfect harmony the Man who is God  
Lord of eternity dwells in humanity  
Kneels in humility and washes our feet  

**Meaning:**  
This stanza marvels at the profound mystery of the Incarnation — Jesus Christ, who is fully God (eternal Lord, majestic deity) and fully man. In perfect balance, the eternal God humbly enters human life, even stooping to wash His disciples’ feet like a servant. It highlights the beautiful union of divine greatness and gentle humility in one Person.

**Chorus**  
O what a mystery meekness and majesty  
Bow down and worship for this is your God  
This is your God  

**Meaning:**  
The chorus invites awe and worship. The combination of meekness (gentle humility) and majesty (divine glory) in Jesus is an unfathomable mystery that should lead us to bow down in adoration, recognising that this humble Man is none other than our God.

**Stanza 2**  
Father's pure radiance perfect in innocence  
Yet learns obedience to death on a cross  
Suffering to give us life  
Conquering through sacrifice  
And as they crucify prays Father forgive  

**Meaning:**  
This part reflects on Christ’s journey to the cross. Though He is the pure, radiant image of the Father and completely innocent, He willingly obeys to the point of death. His suffering brings us life, His sacrifice brings victory, and even while being crucified, He prays for the forgiveness of those who killed Him — showing perfect love and mercy.

**Stanza 3**  
Wisdom unsearchable God the invisible  
Love indestructible in frailty appears  
Lord of infinity stooping so tenderly  
Lifts our humanity to the heights of His throne  

**Meaning:**  
The final stanza celebrates how the invisible, infinite, all-wise God reveals Himself through fragile human flesh. His indestructible love appears in weakness, and in the greatest act of tenderness, He raises fallen humanity up to share in His heavenly glory and throne. It shows God’s desire to exalt us through His own humility.

---

**_Summary of Song_**  
This beautiful worship hymn by Graham Kendrick deeply contemplates the mystery of Jesus Christ — the eternal God who became man, uniting meekness and majesty in perfect harmony. It traces His humble life, sacrificial death on the cross, and ultimate victory, inviting us to bow in worship before this God who stoops low to lift us up to His throne. With its rich theological depth and tender melody, the song inspires awe, gratitude, and wholehearted adoration for the humility and glory of Christ.
