**_Song Meaning_**

**Stanza 1**  
What gift of grace is Jesus my redeemer  
There is no more for heaven now to give  
He is my joy, my righteousness, and freedom  
My steadfast love, my deep and boundless peace  

**Meaning:**  
This verse celebrates Jesus as the greatest gift of God’s grace. Heaven has nothing more to offer because Christ is everything we need — our joy, righteousness, freedom, constant love, and perfect peace. It expresses complete satisfaction in Him alone.

**Chorus**  
To this I hold, my hope is only Jesus  
For my life is wholly bound to His  
Oh how strange and divine, I can sing, "All is mine"  
Yet not I, but through Christ in me  

**Meaning:**  
The singer firmly holds onto the truth that Jesus is his only hope. Because his life is fully united with Christ’s, he can amazingly declare that everything belonging to Christ is his too. The repeated line “Yet not I, but through Christ in me” emphasises that all victory, strength, and blessings come not from self-effort but through Christ living in him.

**Stanza 2**  
The night is dark but I am not forsaken  
For by my side, the Saviour He will stay  
I labour on in weakness and rejoicing  
For in my need, His power is displayed  

**Meaning:**  
Even in dark and difficult times, the believer is never alone because Jesus stays right beside him. He continues working and serving with both weakness and joy, knowing that God’s power shines brightest when we are weak.

**Stanza 3**  
No fate I dread, I know I am forgiven  
The future sure, the price it has been paid  
For Jesus bled and suffered for my pardon  
And He was raised to overthrow the grave  

**Meaning:**  
The singer has no fear of the future because his sins are fully forgiven through Jesus’ death and resurrection. The price for pardon has been completely paid, and death has been defeated, giving unshakable confidence.

**Stanza 4**  
With every breath I long to follow Jesus  
For He has said that He will bring me home  
And day by day I know He will renew me  
Until I stand with joy before the throne  

**Meaning:**  
This verse expresses daily longing to follow Jesus, trusting His promise to bring us safely home to heaven. God renews and strengthens us each day until we stand joyfully before His throne.

**Final Chorus / Ending**  
To this I hold, my hope is only Jesus  
All the glory evermore to Him  
When the race is complete, still my lips shall repeat  
Yet not I, but through Christ in me  

**Meaning:**  
The song closes with a strong declaration of lifelong hope in Jesus alone. All glory belongs to Him. Even after finishing life’s race, the believer will keep declaring that every good thing came “not by my own strength, but through Christ in me.”

---

**_Summary of Song_**  
This powerful modern hymn beautifully expresses the heart of the Christian life: everything we have and everything we become is a gift of God’s grace through Jesus Christ. It takes us through the wonders of salvation, comfort in trials, victory over sin and death, and the hope of eternity, all while repeating the profound truth “Yet not I, but through Christ in me.” The song encourages believers to rest completely in Christ’s finished work and to live each day depending on His strength, giving Him all the glory. It is deeply encouraging for anyone facing darkness, weakness, or the challenges of running the Christian race.
