## Song Meaning

| Stanza | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Verse 1**<br>In Thy presence there's fullness of joy,<br>Fullness of joy, fullness of joy.<br>At Thy right hand are pleasures for ever,<br>Pleasures for evermore. | This verse directly quotes Psalm 16:11, declaring that true and complete joy is found only in God’s presence. At His right hand there are eternal pleasures — a beautiful promise that lasting satisfaction and delight come from being close to the Lord. |
| **Verse 2**<br>I keep the Lord before me,<br>I shall not be moved.<br>My heart is glad and my soul rejoices;<br>I shall dwell in safety. | Drawing from Psalm 16:8-9, this stanza shows the secret to a stable and joyful life: always keeping God in view. When the Lord is our focus, we are not easily shaken, our hearts become glad, and we live with a deep sense of security. |
| **Verse 3**<br>And in Thy presence there's fullness of joy,<br>Fullness of joy, fullness of joy.<br>At Thy right hand are pleasures for ever,<br>Pleasures for evermore. | Repeating the first verse brings the song full circle, strongly reinforcing that the greatest joy and eternal pleasures are found only in God’s presence. The repetition helps the truth sink deep into the heart. |

## Summary of Song

This joyful and uplifting worship song, based on Psalm 16, celebrates the wonderful truth that **fullness of joy** and everlasting pleasures are found in the presence of God. It encourages believers to keep the Lord always before them so their hearts can rejoice and remain secure. With its simple, repeatable structure and biblical foundation, the song creates a beautiful atmosphere for worship, inviting God’s presence and reminding us that true satisfaction is found only in Him. It is perfect for personal devotion, praise and worship services, or anytime we need to refocus on the joy of the Lord.
