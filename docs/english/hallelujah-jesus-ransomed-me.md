## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse 1

> There’s a sweet and blessed story  
> Of the Christ who came from glory,  
> Just to rescue me from sin and misery;  
> He was born in Bethlehem,  
> And in Calvary’s cross condemned,  
> But He died that I might live eternally.

### Chorus

> Hallelujah! Jesus ransomed me,  
> Hallelujah! Jesus ransomed me;  
> He paid a debt I could never pay,  
> Hallelujah! Jesus ransomed me.

### Verse 2

> From the deepest depths of sin,  
> And from the darkest night within,  
> He has lifted me to heights of joy and peace;  
> Now I sing a joyful song,  
> For to Him I do belong,  
> And His blood has brought me full and sweet release.

### Verse 3

> Now I sing redemption’s story,  
> Of the Christ who came from glory,  
> Just to rescue me from sin and misery;  
> He has brought me full salvation,  
> And I stand in His foundation,  
> And I’ll praise Him for all eternity.

## Summary of Song

This joyful redemption hymn celebrates the wonderful story of Jesus who left heaven’s glory to rescue sinners. It powerfully proclaims how Christ paid the unpayable debt through His death on the cross, bringing full salvation, freedom, joy, and peace. With its triumphant “Hallelujah!” chorus, the song stirs gratitude and confident praise for the One who ransomed us. It is ideal for worship services focused on the cross, Easter, or anytime believers want to rejoice in their salvation.
