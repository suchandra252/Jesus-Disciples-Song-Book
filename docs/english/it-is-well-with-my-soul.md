## Line by Line Meaning

**When peace like a river, attendeth my way**  
When life is calm and peaceful, flowing smoothly like a gentle river.

**When sorrows like sea billows roll**  
When troubles come in overwhelming waves, like stormy seas.

**Whatever my lot, Thou hast taught me to say**  
No matter what circumstances God allows in my life.

**It is well, it is well, with my soul**  
I can still declare that my soul is at peace with God.

**It is well (it is well) With my soul (with my soul)**  
Repeated affirmation of deep inner peace with God.

**Though Satan should buffet, though trials should come**  
Even when attacked by the enemy or facing difficult trials.

**Let this blest assurance control**  
Let this comforting truth rule over my heart and mind.

**That Christ has regarded my helpless estate**  
That Jesus saw my helpless, sinful condition.

**And hath shed His own blood for my soul**  
And paid for my sins with His own precious blood.

**My sin, oh, the bliss of this glorious thought!**  
What wonderful joy in this truth!

**My sin, not in part but the whole**  
Every single sin — completely, not partially.

**Is nailed to the cross, and I bear it no more**  
Has been fully dealt with on the cross; I no longer carry the guilt.

**Praise the Lord, praise the Lord, O my soul!**  
So I wholeheartedly praise and worship the Lord!

## Summary of Song

This beloved hymn, written by Horatio Spafford after great personal tragedy, is a profound declaration of faith and peace in God. Despite suffering, loss, and trials, the writer chooses to say “It is well with my soul” because of Christ’s finished work on the cross. With its moving lyrics and comforting melody, the song has brought strength and hope to countless believers facing hardship. It is perfect for times of trial, funeral services, or personal worship when we need to anchor our hearts in God’s faithfulness.
