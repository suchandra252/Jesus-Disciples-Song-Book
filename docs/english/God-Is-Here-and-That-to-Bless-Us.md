**_Song Meaning_**

**Stanza 1**  
God is here, and that to bless us  
With the Spirit’s quickening power;  
See, the cloud already bending,  
Waits to drop the grateful shower.  

**Meaning:**  
This opening stanza boldly declares that God is already present in the meeting and has come specifically to bless His people. It pictures the Holy Spirit’s reviving power as a rain cloud ready to pour down refreshing showers of blessing, building an atmosphere of joyful expectation and faith.

**Chorus**  
Let it come, O Lord, we pray Thee,  
Let the shower of blessings fall;  
We are waiting, we are waiting,  
Oh, revive the hearts of all.  

**Meaning:**  
The chorus is a collective, urgent prayer asking the Lord to send the promised blessing immediately. The repeated “We are waiting” shows eager anticipation and dependence on God, pleading for a fresh revival that will awaken and renew every heart in the congregation.

**Stanza 2**  
God is here! we feel His presence  
In this consecrated place;  
But we need the soul refreshing  
Of His free, unbounded grace.  

**Meaning:**  
Here the singers acknowledge that they can already sense God’s presence in the dedicated place of worship, yet they humbly confess their deep need for a greater inner refreshing. They long for the unlimited, free-flowing grace of the Holy Spirit to satisfy and revive their souls.

**Stanza 3**  
God is here! oh, then, believing,  
Bring to Him our one desire,  
That His love may now be kindled,  
Till it’s flame each heart inspire.  

**Meaning:**  
With faith and confidence that God is present, the stanza calls the people to bring their single, united request before Him: that His love would be stirred up like a fire and set every heart ablaze with passion for the Lord.

**Stanza 4**  
Saviour, grant the prayer we offer,  
While in simple faith we bow,  
From the windows of Thy mercy  
Pour us out a blessing now.  

**Meaning:**  
This closing stanza is a direct, humble plea to Jesus, the Saviour. While bowing in simple childlike faith, the people ask Him to open the “windows of heaven” and pour out His mercy and blessing right at that moment.

---

**_Summary of Song_**  
This classic revival hymn joyfully announces God’s presence in the midst of His people and earnestly pleads for the Holy Spirit to come like refreshing rain, reviving hearts, kindling love, and pouring out abundant blessings. With its simple yet powerful structure and expectant tone, the song creates a perfect atmosphere for corporate worship, inviting the congregation to wait on God together for a fresh outpouring of His grace and power. It is especially meaningful during prayer meetings, worship services, or seasons when the church longs for spiritual renewal and awakening.
