**_Song Meaning_**

**Stanza 1**  
He walked where I walked  
He stood where I stand  
He felt what I feel  
He understands  
He knows my frailty  
Shared my humanity  
Tempted in every way  
Yet without sin  

**Meaning:**  
This beautiful opening powerfully declares the truth of the Incarnation. Jesus did not remain distant — He entered fully into our human experience. He walked the same roads, stood in the same struggles, felt the same emotions, and faced the same temptations we face. Yet, unlike us, He remained completely without sin. He knows our weakness from the inside and still understands us perfectly with compassion.

**Chorus**  
God with us  
So close to us  
God with us  
Emmanuel  
Emmanuel  

**Meaning:**  
The chorus celebrates the heart of the Christmas message and the gospel itself — “God with us.” Jesus is not a far-off God but Emmanuel, God who came near. He is so close that He shares our humanity completely. This truth brings comfort, intimacy, and awe: the holy God chose to dwell among us and remain with us.

**Stanza 2**  
One of a hated race  
Stung by the prejudice  
Suffering injustice  
Yet He forgives  
Wept for my wasted years  
Paid for my wickedness  
He died in my place  
That I might live  

**Meaning:**  
This stanza highlights the depth of Christ’s love and identification with the marginalized and broken. Jesus was born into a despised people, experienced racism and injustice, yet responded with forgiveness. He wept over our wasted, sinful lives and willingly took our punishment upon Himself. His death was substitutionary — He died in our place so that we might have life. This is the gospel in its most personal and moving form.

---

**_Summary of Song_**  
This powerful Graham Kendrick song beautifully captures the wonder of the Incarnation and the depth of Christ’s empathy and love. It reminds us that Jesus didn’t stay in heaven — He walked in our shoes, felt our pain, faced our temptations, experienced injustice, and still chose to die in our place. The repeated cry of “God with us… Emmanuel” anchors the song in the glorious truth that God came near. It is a deeply comforting and worshipful song that draws us to marvel at a Saviour who fully understands us and gave everything to bring us life. Perfect for Christmas, communion, or any time we need to remember how close and compassionate our God truly is.
