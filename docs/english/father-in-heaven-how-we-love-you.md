## 📝 Lyrics

### Verse

> Father in heaven how we love You  
> We lift Your name in all the earth  
> May Your kingdom be established in our praises,  
> As Your people declare Your mighty works!

### Chorus

> Blessed be the Lord God Almighty  
> Who was and is and is to come.  
> Blessed be the Lord God Almighty  
> Who reigns forevermore.

## Summary of Song

This joyful and majestic worship song by Bob Fitts is a heartfelt declaration of love and praise to God the Father. It lifts up His name, longs for His kingdom to be established through worship, and celebrates His eternal reign. With its simple, singable structure and powerful chorus, the song creates a wonderful atmosphere for corporate worship, inviting God’s people to exalt the Lord who was, is, and is to come. It is perfect for praise gatherings, Sunday services, or anytime the church wants to declare God’s greatness.
