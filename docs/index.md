# 🎵 Jesus' Disciples Song Book

*"Speaking to one another with psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody with your heart to the Lord."*
**Ephesians 5:19**

---

## Welcome

Welcome to the **Jesus' Disciples Song Book**, a growing collection of Christ-centered songs, hymns, worship songs, and spiritual melodies.

This online songbook has been created to help believers worship God, meditate on biblical truths, and preserve spiritually rich songs for future generations.

---

## 🔍 Search Songs

Use the **Search** icon in the top-right corner to find songs by:

* Song title
* Lyrics
* Author
* Keywords
* Scripture references

## 📖 Song Categories

### English Songs

Classic hymns, worship songs, gospel songs, and spiritual songs in English.

### Telugu Songs

Telugu worship songs, hymns, and spiritual songs for personal and congregational worship.

## 🌿 Purpose

This songbook seeks to:

* Glorify the Lord Jesus Christ
* Preserve spiritually rich songs
* Encourage personal devotion
* Strengthen congregational worship
* Help believers learn the truths of God's Word through song
